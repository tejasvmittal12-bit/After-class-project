Tribute to A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

About A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

A.P.J Abdul Kalam, the "Missile Man Of India" was born on October 15,1931 in Rameshwaram, Tamil Nadu. He served as the 11th President od India and was a renowed scientist, known for his work in India's space and missile programs.

Achievments

  • Developed India's first sattelite launch vehicle (SLV-III)
  • Led India's successful nuclear tests in 1998
  • Wrote books like "Wing Of Fire" and "India 2020"
  • Recived the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award
